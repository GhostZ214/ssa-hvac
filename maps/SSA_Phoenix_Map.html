Alignment limits

The floor plans are hand-marked photos of schematic drawings that aren't to scale. They were perspective-corrected, then stretched separately in X and Y to fit each building's roof outline (rotation ≈ 0°, since the buildings follow the north-up street grid). Expect room positions to be off by roughly 2–5 m. Snapped positions use real units in the aerial, but which tag belongs to which unit was matched by layout and has not been checked on the roof. Please check tags on site and update this map.
